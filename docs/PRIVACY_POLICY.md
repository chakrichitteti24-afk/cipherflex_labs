## 2. Our Core Privacy Principles

1. **Zero-Trust Architecture:** We implement strict access controls, least-privilege permissions, and continuous authentication across all our data pipelines.
2. **No Selling of Personal Data:** We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or telemetry to data brokers or advertising exchanges.
3. **No Unauthorized AI Model Training:** We do not use proprietary customer data, sensitive health inputs, or private client codebases to train public foundation models without explicit, written, verifiable consent.
4. **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):** Sensitive interactions and data transmissions are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

## 3. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Contact & Inquiry Data:** When you submit an inquiry form or contact our team, we collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, selected subject/topic, and message contents.
- **Client Project Specifications:** For custom engineering engagements, we receive architectural briefs, technical constraints, workflow criteria, and organizational credentials provided under confidentiality agreements.
- **Communications:** Records of correspondence, bug reports, and customer support queries sent to our support channels.

### B. Application-Specific Processing

#### 1. Atlyra — Real-Time AI Interview Copilot & Speech Analytics
- **Audio & Speech Telemetry:** Audio processed during interview preparation or real-time simulation is streamed into memory for transient transcription and latency-sensitive feedback generation.
- **Data Minimization:** Audio buffers are discarded immediately after inference unless you explicitly choose to save an interview transcript to your personal history.
- **Non-Invasive Architecture:** Atlyra is designed to assist candidates with ethical mock interview practice and speech clarity benchmarking.

#### 2. Svanexa AI — Women's Wellness Platform
- **Sensitive Wellness Metrics:** Period cycles, symptom logs, mood logs, and lifestyle markers are protected under heightened security layers.
- **Client-Side & At-Rest Encryption:** Personal wellness records are encrypted with isolated tenant keys.
- **Strict Anonymization:** Analytical telemetry used to improve cycle prediction models is thoroughly scrubbed of personally identifiable information (PII). Health data is never repurposed for marketing or commercial profiling.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- **Device & Connection Data:** IP address (truncated for regional geolocation), browser type and version, operating system, device family, and language preferences.
- **Usage Metrics:** Pages viewed, interaction timestamps, referral sources, and interface responsiveness metrics (collected to ensure system stability and combat malicious DDoS/bot activity).
- **Cookies & Local Storage:** We use strictly necessary local storage items and cookies to maintain security tokens, save UI state (such as dark mode preferences and boot sequences), and prevent CSRF attacks. We do not deploy third-party invasive cross-site advertising trackers.

## 6. How We Protect and Store Your Data

We employ multi-layered technical and organizational cybersecurity measures:
- **Encryption In Transit:** Enforced TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption across all API endpoints, websites, and data tunnels.
- **Encryption At Rest:** Industry-standard AES-256 encryption across databases, backups, and file object stores.
- **Access Segmentation:** Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for internal staff accessing administrative systems.
- **Infrastructure Partners:** Our workloads and databases are hosted on enterprise cloud providers (e.g., Vercel, Supabase, AWS/GCP data centers) maintaining ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and PCI-DSS certifications.
- **Vulnerability Assessments:** Periodic automated dependency scanning (e.g., Oxlint, static code analysis) and prompt security patching.

## 7. Data Retention & Erasure Schedule

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy:
- **General Inquiries:** Retained for up to 12 months after the last communication, after which they are archived or securely deleted.
- **Client Project Artifacts:** Project documentation, contracts, and codebases are maintained for the active duration of the contract plus statutory warranty and limitation periods (typically 3 to 5 years).
- **Application Accounts:** If you delete your account on Atlyra or Svanexa AI, all personal data is deleted from active production databases within 30 days, with complete purge from rotating backups within 90 days.
- **Server Logs:** Security and access logs are retained on a rolling 90-day cycle, after which they are automatically rotated and purged.

## 9. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction (including India, the European Economic Area, the UK, the United States, and other regions), you possess statutory rights over your personal data:

1. **Right to Access & Information:** You have the right to request confirmation of whether we process your data and receive an accessible summary.
2. **Right to Correction & Rectification:** You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may demand the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the original collection purpose or upon withdrawal of consent.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting past lawful processing.
5. **Right to Data Portability:** You may request an export of your personal information in a structured, machine-readable format.
6. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Under India's DPDP Act, you have the right to readily available grievance redressal mechanisms before escalating to statutory authorities.

To exercise any of these rights, email our Grievance Officer at `cipherfluxlabshelp@gmail.com` with the subject line *"Data Privacy Rights Request"*. We evaluate and respond to all verified requests within 30 days.
